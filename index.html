About

My work combines product development, applied analytics, and cross-functional problem-solving. I studied cognitive science and psychology at UC Davis and am pursuing an MBA at Arizona State University. I'm building toward research at the intersection of human behavior and AI systems.

Research Interests

How AI understands and responds to people

How models interpret user intent, balance helpfulness with honesty, and avoid telling people what they want to hear at the expense of accuracy. I'm particularly interested in sycophancy, model behavior, and alignment.

How AI changes human thinking and behavior

How AI affects trust, judgment, decision-making, and consumer behavior—including when people rely on AI appropriately and when they overtrust it.

AI safety and reliable behavior

How to evaluate whether AI systems behave reliably, communicate uncertainty, and support human agency.
